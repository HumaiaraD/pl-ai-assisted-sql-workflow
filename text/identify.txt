Business Goal:
Identify SaaS customer accounts that may be at risk of churn based on both declining product usage and 
recent support activity.

Definition:
An account is at risk if:
- account_status = 'active'
- usage in the last 30 days dropped by 50% or more compared to usage in the previous 30 days
- and the account has at least one high-priority support ticket in the last 30 days

Expected Output:
- account_id
- account_name
- last_30_day_usage
- previous_30_day_usage
- percent_drop
- recent_high_priority_tickets
- risk_reason
This definition makes “at risk” measurable. Instead of leaving the AI to guess, the workflow defines the active 
account status, the 50% usage drop threshold, and recent support friction.
